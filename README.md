##  About

I work at the intersection of **digital assessment, learning experience, and product delivery** — translating regulator, educator, learner, and engineering needs into shippable specs.

For three years (2022–2025) I was the **Curriculum & Learning Experience Designer at UPILY (WonjoKids)**, a children's EdTech startup merging play-based learning with rigorous academic standards. I built a Common Core / Montessori / NGSS–aligned curriculum for ages 3–7, authored learning outcomes that guided developers and game designers, reviewed Game Design Documents (GDDs), prototyped concepts, and led UX playtesting sessions with young children. Working in an Agile setup with Figma, Asana, and Slack taught me how pedagogy and product mechanics shape each other — and **game-based learning, child UX, and curriculum-aligned digital content remain a core area I want to keep working in**.

I then moved into business analysis for a high-stakes online exam and proctoring platform, owning requirements and quality across multiple parallel client projects in regulated assessment.

Before EdTech I spent seven years founding and running an internationally accredited Montessori teacher training academy, which still shapes how I think about pedagogy, curriculum design, and adult learners.

In parallel, I'm pursuing research at the intersection of **knowledge graphs, ontologies, and AI for education** (M.Sc. MIS, Sakarya University).
